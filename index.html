About

Hi, I’m Jerold John Manalili

Multidisciplinary Product Designer based in the Philippines

I design user-centered digital experiences across web, mobile, and complex systems—combining UI/UX, product thinking, and visual design to simplify workflows, improve usability, and drive meaningful business outcomes.

Hello!

With over a decade of experience across UI/UX, web, and digital design, I specialize in creating intuitive and scalable solutions that balance user needs and business goals. My work spans product interfaces, e-commerce platforms, and digital campaigns—allowing me to approach design from both a systems and visual perspective.

I’ve worked on complex applications, mobile platforms, and high-traffic digital products, where I focus on improving usability, streamlining user flows, and delivering consistent, user-friendly experiences. From wireframes to final interfaces, I translate ideas into clear, functional, and visually engaging solutions.

My multidisciplinary background enables me to adapt across different types of projects—whether it’s designing a product experience, optimizing a website, or creating high-impact digital assets. I aim to create designs that not only look refined, but also solve real problems and deliver measurable value.
